# 🎧 Guided Listening 1 – האזנה מודרכת 1

**רמה:** Level 1-2  
**קטגוריה:** האזנה בסיסית

---

## 📋 הוראות

1. **האזן** לתסריט (אם יש הקלטה)
2. **קרא** את התסריט
3. **ענה** על השאלות
4. **בדוק** את התשובות
5. **תרגל** הגייה

---

## 🎯 Listening Exercise 1: Daily Routine

### תסריט

```
Hi! My name is Sarah. I wake up at 7 o'clock every morning.
First, I brush my teeth and take a shower.
Then, I eat breakfast at 8 o'clock. I usually have coffee and toast.
After breakfast, I go to work at 9 o'clock.
I work until 5 o'clock in the afternoon.
I come home at 6 o'clock.
I eat dinner at 7 o'clock.
In the evening, I watch TV or read a book.
I go to bed at 11 o'clock.
```

---

### שאלות

1. What time does Sarah wake up?
   - a) 6 o'clock
   - b) 7 o'clock
   - c) 8 o'clock

2. What does Sarah eat for breakfast?
   - a) Cereal
   - b) Coffee and toast
   - c) Eggs

3. What time does Sarah go to work?
   - a) 8 o'clock
   - b) 9 o'clock
   - c) 10 o'clock

4. What time does Sarah finish work?
   - a) 4 o'clock
   - b) 5 o'clock
   - c) 6 o'clock

5. What does Sarah do in the evening?
   - a) Exercise
   - b) Cook
   - c) Watch TV or read

---

### תשובות

1. b) 7 o'clock
2. b) Coffee and toast
3. b) 9 o'clock
4. b) 5 o'clock
5. c) Watch TV or read

---

## 🎯 Listening Exercise 2: Shopping

### תסריט

```
A: Hello! Can I help you?
B: Yes, please. I'm looking for a gift for my friend.
A: What kind of gift are you looking for?
B: Something nice, maybe a book or a pen.
A: We have some beautiful books over here. What does your friend like?
B: She likes mystery novels.
A: Perfect! We have several mystery novels. This one is very popular.
B: How much is it?
A: It's fifteen dollars.
B: That sounds good. I'll take it.
A: Great! Would you like it wrapped?
B: Yes, please. That would be nice.
A: Here you go. Have a nice day!
B: Thank you! You too!
```

---

### שאלות

1. What is the customer looking for?
   - a) A book for herself
   - b) A gift for a friend
   - c) A pen

2. What does the friend like?
   - a) Romance novels
   - b) Mystery novels
   - c) Science fiction

3. How much does the book cost?
   - a) Ten dollars
   - b) Fifteen dollars
   - c) Twenty dollars

4. Does the customer want the gift wrapped?
   - a) Yes
   - b) No
   - c) Not mentioned

---

### תשובות

1. b) A gift for a friend
2. b) Mystery novels
3. b) Fifteen dollars
4. a) Yes

---

## 🎯 Listening Exercise 3: At the Restaurant

### תסריט

```
Waiter: Good evening! Table for two?
Customer: Yes, please.
Waiter: This way, please. Here's your table.
Customer: Thank you.
Waiter: Here are the menus. Are you ready to order?
Customer: Yes. I'll have the chicken, please.
Waiter: And for you, ma'am?
Customer 2: I'll have the fish, please.
Waiter: And to drink?
Customer: Water for me, please.
Customer 2: I'll have a glass of wine, please.
Waiter: Very good. I'll be back with your order soon.
```

---

### שאלות

1. How many people are at the table?
   - a) One
   - b) Two
   - c) Three

2. What does the first customer order?
   - a) Fish
   - b) Chicken
   - c) Beef

3. What does the second customer order to eat?
   - a) Chicken
   - b) Fish
   - c) Salad

4. What does the first customer order to drink?
   - a) Wine
   - b) Water
   - c) Coffee

---

### תשובות

1. b) Two
2. b) Chicken
3. b) Fish
4. b) Water

---

## 💡 טיפים להאזנה

1. **האזן פעם ראשונה** - ללא קריאה, נסה להבין את הרעיון הכללי
2. **האזן פעם שנייה** - עם קריאה, עקוב אחר המילים
3. **האזן פעם שלישית** - ללא קריאה, נסה להבין כל מילה
4. **תרגל הגייה** - חזור על המשפטים בקול רם
5. **ענה על השאלות** - בדוק את ההבנה שלך
