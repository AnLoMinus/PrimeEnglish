## 📋 הוראות

1. **האזן** לתסריט (אם יש הקלטה)
2. **קרא** את התסריט
3. **ענה** על השאלות
4. **בדוק** את התשובות
5. **תרגל** הגייה

## 🎯 Listening Exercise 1: Travel Plans

### תסריט

```
A: Hi! I heard you're going on a trip. Where are you going?
B: I'm going to Paris next month. I'm really excited!
A: That sounds amazing! How long are you staying?
B: I'm staying for a week. I've never been to Paris before.
A: That's great! Have you booked your hotel yet?
B: Yes, I booked a hotel near the city center. It looks nice.
A: What are you planning to do there?
B: I want to visit the Eiffel Tower, the Louvre Museum, and try some French food.
A: That sounds like a wonderful trip! Have a great time!
B: Thank you! I'm sure I will.
```

---

### שאלות

1. Where is the person going?
   - a) London
   - b) Paris
   - c) Rome

2. How long is the trip?
   - a) Three days
   - b) One week
   - c) Two weeks

3. Has the person been to Paris before?
   - a) Yes
   - b) No
   - c) Not mentioned

4. What does the person want to do? (Choose all that apply)
   - a) Visit the Eiffel Tower
   - b) Visit the Louvre Museum
   - c) Try French food
   - d) All of the above

---

### תשובות

1. b) Paris
2. b) One week
3. b) No
4. d) All of the above

## 🎯 Listening Exercise 2: Job Interview

### תסריט

```
Interviewer: Good morning! Please, have a seat.
Candidate: Good morning. Thank you.
Interviewer: So, tell me about yourself.
Candidate: Well, I've been working as a teacher for five years. I really enjoy working with students.
Interviewer: That's great. Why do you want to work here?
Candidate: I've heard great things about this school, and I think I can contribute a lot.
Interviewer: What are your strengths?
Candidate: I'm patient, organized, and I love teaching. I also speak three languages.
Interviewer: That's impressive. Do you have any questions for us?
Candidate: Yes. What opportunities are there for professional development?
Interviewer: We offer regular training and workshops. Is there anything else?
Candidate: No, that's all. Thank you for your time.
Interviewer: Thank you for coming. We'll be in touch soon.
```

---

### שאלות

1. How long has the candidate been working as a teacher?
   - a) Three years
   - b) Five years
   - c) Seven years

2. How many languages does the candidate speak?
   - a) One
   - b) Two
   - c) Three

3. What does the candidate ask about?
   - a) Salary
   - b) Professional development
   - c) Vacation time

4. What will the interviewer do next?
   - a) Offer the job immediately
   - b) Be in touch soon
   - c) Schedule another interview

---

### תשובות

1. b) Five years
2. c) Three
3. b) Professional development
4. b) Be in touch soon

## 🎯 Listening Exercise 3: Health Discussion

### תסריט

```
Doctor: Good morning. How can I help you today?
Patient: Good morning, doctor. I haven't been feeling well lately.
Doctor: What seems to be the problem?
Patient: I've been having headaches and I feel tired all the time.
Doctor: How long have you had these symptoms?
Patient: About two weeks now.
Doctor: Are you getting enough sleep?
Patient: I try to sleep eight hours, but I wake up tired.
Doctor: How about exercise? Do you exercise regularly?
Patient: Not really. I'm quite busy with work.
Doctor: I see. Let me check your blood pressure. It's a bit high.
Patient: Is that serious?
Doctor: Not too serious, but we should address it. I recommend more exercise and less stress.
Patient: I'll try. Thank you, doctor.
Doctor: You're welcome. Come back in a month for a check-up.
```

---

### שאלות

1. What are the patient's symptoms?
   - a) Headaches and tiredness
   - b) Fever and cough
   - c) Stomach pain

2. How long has the patient had these symptoms?
   - a) One week
   - b) Two weeks
   - c) Three weeks

3. What does the doctor find?
   - a) Low blood pressure
   - b) Normal blood pressure
   - c) High blood pressure

4. What does the doctor recommend?
   - a) More exercise and less stress
   - b) More sleep
   - c) Medication

---

### תשובות

1. a) Headaches and tiredness
2. b) Two weeks
3. c) High blood pressure
4. a) More exercise and less stress

## 💡 טיפים להאזנה בינונית

1. **האזן לפרטים** - לא רק לרעיון הכללי
2. **שימו לב לזמנים** - עבר, הווה, עתיד
3. **הבינו הקשר** - מה הקשר בין הדוברים?
4. **תרגלו מילים חדשות** - כתבו מילים שלא הכרתם
5. **חזרו על זה** - האזינו מספר פעמים
