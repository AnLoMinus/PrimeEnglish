# 🎧 Listening Scripts – תסריטי האזנה

**רמה:** כל הרמות  
**קטגוריה:** תרגול האזנה

---

## 📋 איך להשתמש

1. **האזן** לתסריט (אם יש הקלטה)
2. **קרא** את התסריט
3. **השווה** מה ששמעת למה שכתוב
4. **תרגל** הגייה
5. **חזור** על התהליך

---

## 🟢 Level 1: Basic Listening

### Script 1: Introducing Yourself

**תסריט:**
```
A: Hello! My name is David. What's your name?
B: Hi David! I'm Sarah. Nice to meet you.
A: Nice to meet you too, Sarah!
B: Where are you from, David?
A: I'm from Tel Aviv. And you?
B: I'm from Jerusalem. That's nice!
A: Yes, it is. Have a nice day!
B: You too! Goodbye!
```

**שאלות:**
1. What is David's name?
2. Where is Sarah from?
3. Where is David from?

**תשובות:**
1. David
2. Jerusalem
3. Tel Aviv

---

### Script 2: At a Store

**תסריט:**
```
A: Hello! Can I help you?
B: Yes, please. How much is this shirt?
A: It's twenty dollars.
B: Do you have it in blue?
A: Yes, we do. What size?
B: Medium, please.
A: Here you go.
B: Thank you!
A: You're welcome!
```

**שאלות:**
1. How much is the shirt?
2. What color does the customer want?
3. What size does the customer want?

**תשובות:**
1. Twenty dollars
2. Blue
3. Medium

## 🔵 Level 2: Intermediate Listening

### Script 3: At a Restaurant

**תסריט:**
```
Waiter: Good evening! Table for two?
Customer: Yes, please.
Waiter: This way, please.
Waiter: Are you ready to order?
Customer: Yes. I'll have the chicken, please.
Waiter: And to drink?
Customer: Water, please.
Waiter: Anything else?
Customer: No, that's all. Thank you.
Waiter: I'll be back with your order soon.
```

**שאלות:**
1. How many people are at the table?
2. What does the customer order to eat?
3. What does the customer order to drink?

**תשובות:**
1. Two
2. Chicken
3. Water

### Script 4: Asking for Directions

**תסריט:**
```
A: Excuse me, where is the train station?
B: Go straight for two blocks, then turn left.
A: Is it far?
B: No, it's about five minutes walk.
A: Thank you very much!
B: You're welcome!
```

**שאלות:**
1. Where does the person want to go?
2. How should they get there?
3. How long does it take?

**תשובות:**
1. Train station
2. Go straight for two blocks, then turn left
3. About five minutes walk

## 🟣 Level 3: Advanced Listening

### Script 5: Business Meeting

**תסריט:**
```
A: Good morning, everyone. Thank you for coming.
B: Good morning. We're excited to hear your proposal.
A: Great! Let me start by outlining our approach.
B: Please, go ahead.
A: We've analyzed the market and identified three key opportunities.
B: That's interesting. Can you elaborate?
A: Certainly. First, there's a growing demand in the tech sector.
B: What's your timeline for implementation?
A: We're looking at a six-month rollout, starting in Q2.
B: And what about the budget?
A: We've estimated around $500,000 for the initial phase.
B: I see. Let's discuss the details after the presentation.
```

**שאלות:**
1. How many opportunities were identified?
2. What is the timeline?
3. What is the estimated budget?

**תשובות:**
1. Three
2. Six months, starting in Q2
3. Around $500,000

### Script 6: Academic Discussion

**תסריט:**
```
A: I've been reading your latest paper on cognitive psychology.
B: Oh, really? What did you think?
A: It's fascinating. Your methodology is quite innovative.
B: Thank you. We spent a lot of time developing it.
A: I'm particularly interested in your findings on memory retention.
B: Yes, those results were quite surprising.
A: Have you considered applying this to educational settings?
B: That's actually our next research direction.
A: I'd love to collaborate on that if you're interested.
B: That sounds like a great idea. Let's discuss it further.
```

**שאלות:**
1. What is the paper about?
2. What findings is person A interested in?
3. What is the next research direction?

**תשובות:**
1. Cognitive psychology
2. Memory retention
3. Educational settings

## 🔴 Level 4: Professional Listening

### Script 7: Legal Consultation

**תסריט:**
```
Lawyer: Good morning. How can I help you today?
Client: Good morning. I need advice on a contract dispute.
Lawyer: I see. Can you tell me more about the situation?
Client: We signed a contract six months ago, but the other party isn't fulfilling their obligations.
Lawyer: What does the contract specify?
Client: They were supposed to deliver the goods by the end of last month.
Lawyer: And have you contacted them about this?
Client: Yes, multiple times, but they keep delaying.
Lawyer: Do you have documentation of these communications?
Client: Yes, I have all the emails and letters.
Lawyer: Good. Based on what you've told me, you may have grounds for legal action.
Client: What would that involve?
Lawyer: We could send a formal notice first, and if that doesn't work, file a lawsuit.
```

**שאלות:**
1. What is the problem?
2. When were the goods supposed to be delivered?
3. What is the first step in legal action?

**תשובות:**
1. The other party isn't fulfilling their obligations
2. By the end of last month
3. Send a formal notice

## 🟡 Level 5: Mastery Listening

### Script 8: Research Presentation

**תסריט:**
```
Good morning, everyone. Thank you for being here today.
I'm going to present our research findings on the effects of meditation on cognitive function.
We conducted a study with 200 participants over six months.
Our methodology involved daily meditation sessions and cognitive assessments.
The results were quite remarkable. We found significant improvements in memory, attention, and problem-solving skills.
Furthermore, these improvements were maintained even after the study ended.
However, we should note some limitations. The sample size, while substantial, was not diverse enough.
Future research should explore different populations and longer time frames.
In conclusion, our findings suggest that regular meditation can have lasting positive effects on cognitive function.
Thank you for your attention. I'm happy to answer any questions.
```

**שאלות:**
1. How many participants were in the study?
2. What improvements were found?
3. What are the limitations mentioned?

**תשובות:**
1. 200
2. Memory, attention, and problem-solving skills
3. Sample size not diverse enough

5. **תרגל עם חבר** - תרגל דיאלוגים
