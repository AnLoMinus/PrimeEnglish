# 🏗️ Sentence Builder – בונה משפטים

**רמה:** כל הרמות  
**קטגוריה:** תרגול בניית משפטים

---

## 📝 איך להשתמש

1. **בחר** רמה
2. **קרא** את המילים
3. **בנה** משפט
4. **בדוק** את התשובה
5. **תרגל** שוב

---

## 🟢 Level 1: Simple Sentences

### תרגיל 1: Basic Structure

**מילים:**
- I, like, coffee

**תשובה:**
- I like coffee.

---

### תרגיל 2: With Adjectives

**מילים:**
- The, car, is, red

**תשובה:**
- The car is red.

---

### תרגיל 3: Questions

**מילים:**
- What, is, this

**תשובה:**
- What is this?

---

### תרגיל 4: Negative

**מילים:**
- I, do, not, like, tea

**תשובה:**
- I do not like tea. / I don't like tea.

---

### תרגיל 5: With "To Be"

**מילים:**
- She, is, a, teacher

**תשובה:**
- She is a teacher.

---

## 🔵 Level 2: Past & Present

### תרגיל 6: Past Simple

**מילים:**
- I, went, to, the, store, yesterday

**תשובה:**
- I went to the store yesterday.

---

### תרגיל 7: Present Continuous

**מילים:**
- She, is, reading, a, book, now

**תשובה:**
- She is reading a book now.

---

### תרגיל 8: Questions in Past

**מילים:**
- Did, you, go, to, school, yesterday

**תשובה:**
- Did you go to school yesterday?

---

### תרגיל 9: With Time

**מילים:**
- I, work, every, day, at, 9, AM

**תשובה:**
- I work every day at 9 AM.

---

### תרגיל 10: Complex

**מילים:**
- Where, did, you, go, last, week

**תשובה:**
- Where did you go last week?

---

## 🟣 Level 3: Present Perfect

### תרגיל 11: Present Perfect

**מילים:**
- I, have, finished, my, homework

**תשובה:**
- I have finished my homework.

---

### תרגיל 12: With "For"

**מילים:**
- I, have, lived, here, for, five, years

**תשובה:**
- I have lived here for five years.

---

### תרגיל 13: With "Since"

**מילים:**
- She, has, worked, here, since, 2020

**תשובה:**
- She has worked here since 2020.

---

### תרגיל 14: Questions

**מילים:**
- Have, you, ever, been, to, Paris

**תשובה:**
- Have you ever been to Paris?

---

### תרגיל 15: Comparatives

**מילים:**
- This, car, is, bigger, than, that, one

**תשובה:**
- This car is bigger than that one.

---

## 🔴 Level 4: Conditionals

### תרגיל 16: First Conditional

**מילים:**
- If, it, rains, I, will, stay, home

**תשובה:**
- If it rains, I will stay home.

---

### תרגיל 17: Second Conditional

**מילים:**
- If, I, won, the, lottery, I, would, travel

**תשובה:**
- If I won the lottery, I would travel.

---

### תרגיל 18: Passive Voice

**מילים:**
- The, letter, was, written, yesterday

**תשובה:**
- The letter was written yesterday.

---

### תרגיל 19: Relative Clause

**מילים:**
- The, man, who, called, is, my, friend

**תשובה:**
- The man who called is my friend.

---

### תרגיל 20: Complex

**מילים:**
- Although, it, was, raining, we, went, out

**תשובה:**
- Although it was raining, we went out.

---

## 🟡 Level 5: Advanced Structures

### תרגיל 21: Mixed Conditional

**מילים:**
- If, I, had, studied, more, I, would, be, smarter, now

**תשובה:**
- If I had studied more, I would be smarter now.

---

### תרגיל 22: Inversion

**מילים:**
- Never, have, I, seen, such, a, thing

**תשובה:**
- Never have I seen such a thing.

---

### תרגיל 23: Cleft Sentence

**מילים:**
- It, is, a, book, that, I, want

**תשובה:**
- It is a book that I want.

---

### תרגיל 24: Academic

**מילים:**
- It, is, widely, acknowledged, that, this, is, important

**תשובה:**
- It is widely acknowledged that this is important.

---

### תרגיל 25: Complex Academic

**מילים:**
- The, research, findings, suggest, that, further, investigation, is, needed

**תשובה:**
- The research findings suggest that further investigation is needed.

---

## 🎯 תרגילים מותאמים אישית

### בנה משפט משלך

**בחר נושא:**
- Daily routine
- Work
- Travel
- Food
- Hobbies

**כתוב 5 משפטים בנושא שבחרת**

---

## 📊 דוגמאות לפי נושא

### Daily Routine

1. I wake up at 7 AM every day.
2. I brush my teeth and take a shower.
3. I eat breakfast at 8 AM.
4. I go to work at 9 AM.
5. I come home at 6 PM.

---

### Work

1. I work as a teacher.
2. My office is in the city center.
3. I have meetings every Monday.
4. I finish work at 5 PM.
5. I enjoy my job very much.

---

### Travel

1. I traveled to Paris last year.
2. I stayed in a hotel for three nights.
3. I visited many famous places.
4. I took many photos.
5. I had a wonderful time.

---

## 💡 טיפים לבניית משפטים

1. **התחל פשוט** - משפטים קצרים תחילה
2. **הוסף פרטים** - הוסף מילים תיאוריות
3. **השתמש בזמנים נכונים** - עבר, הווה, עתיד
4. **תרגל כל יום** - עקביות חשובה
5. **קרא הרבה** - קרא כדי לראות דוגמאות
