# 📚 Advanced Grammar – דקדוק מתקדם

**רמה:** Level 4  
**קטגוריה:** דקדוק מתקדם

---

## 🔀 Conditionals (תנאים)

### Zero Conditional (עובדות כלליות)

**מבנה:** If + Present Simple, Present Simple

**שימוש:** עובדות, חוקי טבע

**דוגמאות:**
- If you **heat** water, it **boils**.
- If it **rains**, the ground **gets** wet.
- If you **mix** red and blue, you **get** purple.

---

### First Conditional (סביר)

**מבנה:** If + Present Simple, will + verb

**שימוש:** מצבים סביריים בעתיד

**דוגמאות:**
- If it **rains**, I **will stay** home.
- If you **study**, you **will pass**.
- If she **comes**, we **will go** to the cinema.

---

### Second Conditional (לא סביר/דמיוני)

**מבנה:** If + Past Simple, would + verb

**שימוש:** מצבים לא סביריים או דמיוניים

**דוגמאות:**
- If I **won** the lottery, I **would travel** the world.
- If I **were** you, I **would go**.
- If she **had** time, she **would help**.

**הערה:** עם "I" ו-"he/she/it" משתמשים ב-"were" ולא "was"

---

### Third Conditional (עבר, לא קרה)

**מבנה:** If + Past Perfect, would have + past participle

**שימוש:** מצבים בעבר שלא קרו

**דוגמאות:**
- If I **had studied**, I **would have passed**.
- If she **had come**, we **would have had** fun.
- If they **had left** earlier, they **would have arrived** on time.

---

## 🔄 Passive Voice (צילום סביל)

### שימוש

1. **כשהפעולה חשובה יותר מהעושה:**
   - The food **is cooked** by the chef.

2. **כשהעושה לא ידוע:**
   - My car **was stolen**.

3. **בכתיבה אקדמית ומדעית:**
   - The experiment **was conducted** last year.

---

### צורות לפי זמנים

**Present Simple:**
- Active: The chef **cooks** the food.
- Passive: The food **is cooked** by the chef.

**Past Simple:**
- Active: They **built** this house in 1990.
- Passive: This house **was built** in 1990.

**Present Continuous:**
- Active: They **are building** a new bridge.
- Passive: A new bridge **is being built**.

**Past Continuous:**
- Active: They **were building** a bridge.
- Passive: A bridge **was being built**.

**Present Perfect:**
- Active: Someone **has stolen** my car.
- Passive: My car **has been stolen**.

**Past Perfect:**
- Active: They **had finished** the work.
- Passive: The work **had been finished**.

**Future:**
- Active: They **will build** a new school.
- Passive: A new school **will be built**.

**Modal:**
- Active: You **must do** this.
- Passive: This **must be done**.

---

### דוגמאות

**Present:**
- The letter **is written**.
- The books **are sold** here.

**Past:**
- The letter **was written**.
- The books **were sold** yesterday.

**Future:**
- The letter **will be written**.
- The books **will be sold** next week.

**Present Perfect:**
- The letter **has been written**.
- The books **have been sold**.

---

## 🔗 Complex Structures (מבנים מורכבים)

### Relative Clauses (פסוקיות יחס)

**Who** - לאנשים:
- The man **who** called is my friend.
- The people **who** live here are nice.

**Which** - לדברים:
- The book **which** I read was good.
- The car **which** he bought is expensive.

**That** - לאנשים ודברים:
- The book **that** I read was good.
- The man **that** called is my friend.

**Where** - למקומות:
- The place **where** we met is closed.
- This is the house **where** I grew up.

**Whose** - לקניין:
- The man **whose** car was stolen is angry.
- The student **whose** book I borrowed is here.

**When** - לזמן:
- I remember the day **when** we met.
- The time **when** it happened was strange.

---

### Defining vs. Non-Defining Clauses

**Defining** (מגדיר, ללא פסיקים):
- The students **who study hard** pass the exam.
- The car **that I bought** is red.

**Non-Defining** (לא מגדיר, עם פסיקים):
- My brother, **who lives in Paris**, is a doctor.
- The book, **which I read last week**, was interesting.

---

### Reported Speech (דיבור עקיף)

**שינויים בזמנים:**

| Direct | Reported |
|--------|----------|
| Present Simple | Past Simple |
| Present Continuous | Past Continuous |
| Present Perfect | Past Perfect |
| Past Simple | Past Perfect |
| Will | Would |
| Can | Could |
| Must | Had to |

**דוגמאות:**

**Direct:** "I'm tired," she said.  
**Reported:** She said **that** she **was** tired.

**Direct:** "I will come," he said.  
**Reported:** He said **that** he **would** come.

**Direct:** "I have finished," they said.  
**Reported:** They said **that** they **had finished**.

**Direct:** "I can help," she said.  
**Reported:** She said **that** she **could** help.

---

### Gerunds & Infinitives (שם פועל וצורת המקור)

**Gerund (-ing):**
- I enjoy **reading** books.
- She likes **swimming**.
- They stopped **smoking**.

**Infinitive (to + verb):**
- I want **to read** this book.
- She needs **to study**.
- They decided **to go**.

**שניהם אפשריים (עם הבדל משמעות):**
- I stopped **smoking**. (הפסקתי לעשן)
- I stopped **to smoke**. (עצרתי כדי לעשן)

**רשימת פעלים עם Gerund:**
- enjoy, like, love, hate, prefer, avoid, finish, stop, suggest

**רשימת פעלים עם Infinitive:**
- want, need, decide, plan, hope, expect, agree, refuse, offer

## 📊 Advanced Tenses (זמנים מתקדמים)

### Past Perfect (עבר מושלם)

**שימוש:** פעולה שהסתיימה לפני פעולה אחרת בעבר

**דוגמאות:**
- When I arrived, the meeting **had already started**.
- She **had finished** her work before I came.
- They **had left** by the time we arrived.

---

### Future Perfect (עתיד מושלם)

**שימוש:** פעולה שתסתיים לפני זמן מסוים בעתיד

**דוגמאות:**
- By next year, I **will have finished** my studies.
- She **will have arrived** by 5 PM.
- They **will have completed** the project by Friday.

---

### Future Continuous (עתיד ממושך)

**שימוש:** פעולה שתקרה בזמן מסוים בעתיד

**דוגמאות:**
- At 3 PM tomorrow, I **will be working**.
- This time next week, we **will be traveling**.
- She **will be studying** when you arrive.

---

## 🎯 Advanced Modals (פעלי עזר מתקדמים)

### Must vs. Have to

**Must** - חובה אישית:
- I **must** finish this today. (אני מרגיש שצריך)

**Have to** - חובה חיצונית:
- I **have to** work tomorrow. (הבוס שלי דורש)

---

### Should vs. Ought to

**Should** - מומלץ:
- You **should** study more.

**Ought to** - מומלץ (יותר פורמלי):
- You **ought to** see a doctor.

---

### May vs. Might

**May** - אפשרות (יותר סביר):
- It **may** rain. (50% סיכוי)

**Might** - אפשרות (פחות סביר):
- It **might** rain. (30% סיכוי)

---

## 💡 Common Mistakes (שגיאות נפוצות)

### 1. Conditionals

❌ **שגוי:** "If I was you"  
✅ **נכון:** "If I **were** you" (Second Conditional)

---

### 2. Passive Voice

❌ **שגוי:** "The car was stole"  
✅ **נכון:** "The car **was stolen**" (past participle)

---

### 3. Reported Speech

❌ **שגוי:** "She said that she is tired" (אם זה בעבר)  
✅ **נכון:** "She said that she **was** tired"

## 📚 תרגילים

### תרגיל 1: Conditionals

1. If it ___ (rain), I ___ (stay) home.
2. If I ___ (win) the lottery, I ___ (travel) the world.
3. If I ___ (study), I ___ (pass) the exam.

### תרגיל 2: Passive Voice

1. The letter ___ (write) yesterday.
2. The books ___ (sell) here.
3. A new bridge ___ (build) next year.

### תרגיל 3: Reported Speech

1. "I'm tired," she said. → She said that...
2. "I will come," he said. → He said that...
3. "I have finished," they said. → They said that...
