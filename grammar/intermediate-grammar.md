# 📚 Intermediate Grammar – דקדוק בינוני

**רמה:** Level 2-3  
**קטגוריה:** דקדוק בינוני

---

## ⏳ Present Continuous (הווה ממושך)

### שימוש

1. **פעולות שקורות עכשיו:**
   - I **am reading** a book now.
   - She **is working** right now.

2. **תוכניות עתידיות:**
   - We **are going** to the cinema tonight.
   - They **are meeting** tomorrow.

3. **שינויים זמניים:**
   - I **am staying** with my friend this week.

---

### צורות

**חיובי:**
- I **am** + verb-ing
- You/We/They **are** + verb-ing
- He/She/It **is** + verb-ing

**שלילה:**
- I **am not** + verb-ing
- You/We/They **are not** + verb-ing
- He/She/It **is not** + verb-ing

**שאלות:**
- **Am** I + verb-ing?
- **Are** you/we/they + verb-ing?
- **Is** he/she/it + verb-ing?

---

### דוגמאות

**חיובי:**
- I **am studying** English.
- She **is cooking** dinner.
- They **are playing** football.

**שלילה:**
- I **am not** sleeping.
- She **is not** working.
- They **are not** coming.

**שאלות:**
- **Are** you **listening**?
- **Is** she **coming**?
- **What are** you **doing**?

---

## ⏮️ Past Simple (עבר פשוט)

### שימוש

1. **פעולות שהסתיימו בעבר:**
   - I **went** to the store yesterday.
   - She **worked** here last year.

2. **אירועים בעבר:**
   - They **played** football last week.
   - We **ate** pizza yesterday.

---

### צורות

**פעלים רגילים:**
- work → worked
- play → played
- study → studied

**פעלים לא רגילים:**
- go → went
- see → saw
- eat → ate
- have → had
- do → did
- come → came
- take → took
- make → made

---

### שלילה ושאלות

**שלילה:**
- I **didn't** go.
- She **didn't** work.
- They **didn't** come.

**שאלות:**
- **Did** you **go**?
- **Did** she **work**?
- **What did** you **do**?

---

### מילות זמן

- yesterday - אתמול
- last week - השבוע שעבר
- last month - החודש שעבר
- last year - השנה שעברה
- ago - לפני
- in 2020 - ב-2020

---

## ✅ Present Perfect (הווה מושלם)

### שימוש

1. **פעולות שהתחילו בעבר ונמשכות עד עכשיו:**
   - I **have lived** here for five years.
   - She **has worked** here since 2020.

2. **פעולות שהסתיימו בעבר אבל התוצאה רלוונטית עכשיו:**
   - I **have finished** my homework.
   - They **have arrived**.

3. **ניסיון חיים:**
   - I **have been** to Paris.
   - She **has seen** this movie.

---

### צורות

**חיובי:**
- I/You/We/They **have** + past participle
- He/She/It **has** + past participle

**שלילה:**
- I/You/We/They **haven't** + past participle
- He/She/It **hasn't** + past participle

**שאלות:**
- **Have** you/we/they + past participle?
- **Has** he/she/it + past participle?

---

### דוגמאות

**חיובי:**
- I **have seen** this movie.
- She **has finished** her work.
- They **have been** to London.

**שלילה:**
- I **haven't** seen him today.
- She **hasn't** finished yet.
- They **haven't** arrived.

**שאלות:**
- **Have** you **been** to Paris?
- **Has** she **finished**?
- **How long have** you **lived** here?

---

### מילות זמן

- **already** - כבר
- **yet** - עדיין (בשאלות ושלילה)
- **just** - רק עכשיו
- **ever** - אי פעם
- **never** - מעולם לא
- **for** - במשך (זמן)
- **since** - מאז (נקודת זמן)

**דוגמאות:**
- I **have already** finished.
- **Have** you **ever** been to Japan?
- I **have never** seen that.
- I **have lived** here **for** five years.
- I **have worked** here **since** 2020.

---

## 🔮 Future (עתיד)

### Will (עתיד פשוט)

**שימוש:**
- החלטות ספונטניות
- תחזיות
- הבטחות

**צורות:**
- I/You/He/She/It/We/They **will** + verb

**דוגמאות:**
- I **will** help you.
- She **will** come tomorrow.
- They **will** be here soon.

**שלילה:**
- I **won't** (will not) go.

**שאלות:**
- **Will** you come?

---

### Going to (תוכניות)

**שימוש:**
- תוכניות עתידיות
- כוונות

**צורות:**
- I **am going to** + verb
- You/We/They **are going to** + verb
- He/She/It **is going to** + verb

**דוגמאות:**
- I **am going to** study tonight.
- She **is going to** travel next month.
- They **are going to** buy a car.

---

## 🎯 Modal Verbs (פעלי עזר)

### Can / Could

**Can** - יכולת, אפשרות:
- I **can** speak English.
- **Can** I help you?

**Could** - יכולת בעבר, בקשה מנומסת:
- I **could** swim when I was young.
- **Could** you help me?

---

### Should / Must

**Should** - צריך, מומלץ:
- You **should** study more.
- I **should** go now.

**Must** - חובה:
- You **must** be careful.
- I **must** finish this today.

---

### May / Might

**May** - רשות, אפשרות:
- **May** I come in?
- It **may** rain.

**Might** - אפשרות (פחות ודאי):
- It **might** rain.
- She **might** come.

---

### Will / Would

**Will** - עתיד, הבטחה:
- I **will** help you.
- She **will** come.

**Would** - בקשה מנומסת, תנאי:
- **Would** you like coffee?
- If I had time, I **would** go.

---

## 📊 Comparatives & Superlatives (השוואות)

### Comparatives (השוואה)

**תואר הפועל קצר:**
- big → **bigger**
- small → **smaller**
- fast → **faster**

**תואר הפועל ארוך:**
- beautiful → **more beautiful**
- interesting → **more interesting**

**פעלים לא רגילים:**
- good → **better**
- bad → **worse**
- many/much → **more**
- little → **less**

**דוגמאות:**
- This car is **bigger** than that one.
- She is **more intelligent** than him.
- This is **better** than that.

---

### Superlatives (השוואה עליונה)

**תואר הפועל קצר:**
- big → **biggest**
- small → **smallest**
- fast → **fastest**

**תואר הפועל ארוך:**
- beautiful → **most beautiful**
- interesting → **most interesting**

**פעלים לא רגילים:**
- good → **best**
- bad → **worst**
- many/much → **most**
- little → **least**

**דוגמאות:**
- This is the **biggest** car.
- She is the **most intelligent** person.
- This is the **best** restaurant.

## 📝 Question Forms (צורות שאלה)

### Yes/No Questions

- **Do** you like coffee?
- **Does** she work here?
- **Did** you go yesterday?
- **Are** you ready?
- **Is** she coming?
- **Have** you finished?
- **Can** you help me?

---

- **Where** did you go?
- **When** does it start?
- **Who** is that?

- **How** do you do that?

---

- **How many** people are there?
- **How often** do you go?
- **How long** does it take?
- **How far** is it?

## 💡 Common Mistakes (שגיאות נפוצות)

### 1. Present Perfect vs. Past Simple

❌ **שגוי:** "I have seen him yesterday"  
✅ **נכון:** "I **saw** him yesterday" (Past Simple)

❌ **שגוי:** "I saw this movie" (כשהתוצאה רלוונטית)  
✅ **נכון:** "I **have seen** this movie" (Present Perfect)

---

### 2. Will vs. Going to

❌ **שגוי:** "I will go to Paris next month" (תוכנית)  
✅ **נכון:** "I **am going to** go to Paris next month"

---

### 3. Comparatives

❌ **שגוי:** "This is more good"  
✅ **נכון:** "This is **better**"

---

## 📚 תרגילים

1. I ___ (see) this movie.
