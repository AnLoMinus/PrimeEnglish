# 📚 Mastery Grammar Structures – מבני שליטה

**רמה:** Level 5  
**קטגוריה:** מבנים מתקדמים ביותר

---

## 🎯 Advanced Conditionals (תנאים מתקדמים)

### Mixed Conditionals (תנאים מעורבים)

**עבר → הווה:**
- If I **had studied** harder, I **would be** smarter now.
- If she **had taken** that job, she **would be** rich today.

**הווה → עבר:**
- If I **were** smarter, I **would have passed** that exam.
- If he **knew** the answer, he **would have told** us.

---

### Inverted Conditionals (תנאים הפוכים)

**ללא "if":**
- **Had** I known, I would have come.
- **Were** I you, I would go.
- **Should** you need help, call me.

---

## 🔄 Advanced Passive (צילום סביל מתקדם)

### Passive with Two Objects

**Active:** They gave **me** a **book**.  
**Passive 1:** I **was given** a book.  
**Passive 2:** A book **was given** to me.

---

### Passive Infinitive

- The work needs **to be done**.
- The report is expected **to be completed** soon.
- She wants **to be invited** to the party.

---

### Passive Gerund

- I don't like **being criticized**.
- She enjoys **being praised**.
- They avoid **being seen** together.

---

## 📝 Advanced Relative Clauses (פסוקיות יחס מתקדמות)

### Reduced Relative Clauses

**מלא:**
- The people **who are waiting** outside are students.

**מקוצר:**
- The people **waiting** outside are students.

**מלא:**
- The book **that was written** by him is famous.

**מקוצר:**
- The book **written** by him is famous.

---

### Prepositions in Relative Clauses

**Formal:**
- The house **in which** I grew up is old.
- The person **with whom** I work is nice.

**Informal:**
- The house **which** I grew up **in** is old.
- The person **who** I work **with** is nice.

---

## 🎭 Subjunctive Mood (תחביר דמיוני)

### Subjunctive with "wish"

**הווה:**
- I wish I **were** rich. (אבל אני לא)
- I wish she **were** here. (אבל היא לא)

**עבר:**
- I wish I **had studied** more. (אבל לא למדתי)
- I wish she **had come**. (אבל היא לא באה)

**עתיד:**
- I wish you **would** help me. (אבל אתה לא עוזר)

---

### Subjunctive with "if only"

- **If only** I **were** rich!
- **If only** I **had studied** more!
- **If only** you **would** listen!

---

## 🔗 Advanced Connectors (חיבורים מתקדמים)

### Contrast (ניגוד)

- **Although** / **Though** / **Even though** it rained, we went out.
- **Despite** / **In spite of** the rain, we went out.
- **However**, it was still fun.
- **Nevertheless**, we enjoyed it.
- **On the other hand**, some people stayed home.

---

### Cause and Effect (סיבה ותוצאה)

- **Because** / **Since** / **As** it was raining, we stayed home.
- **Due to** / **Owing to** the rain, we stayed home.
- **Therefore** / **Consequently** / **As a result**, we didn't go out.
- **So** we stayed home.

---

### Addition (הוספה)

- **Furthermore** / **Moreover** / **In addition**, it was cold.
- **Besides**, we were tired.
- **Not only** was it raining, **but also** it was cold.

---

## 📊 Inversion (היפוך)

### Negative Inversion

**רגיל:**
- I have **never** seen such a thing.

**היפוך:**
- **Never have** I seen such a thing.

**דוגמאות נוספות:**
- **Seldom do** I eat fast food.
- **Rarely have** I been so surprised.
- **Not only did** he come, **but also** he helped.
- **Hardly had** I arrived when it started raining.
- **No sooner had** I left than it started raining.

---

### Conditional Inversion

**רגיל:**
- If I had known, I would have come.

**היפוך:**
- **Had** I known, I would have come.

---

## 🎯 Cleft Sentences (משפטי פיצול)

### It-cleft

**רגיל:**
- I want a book.

**פיצול:**
- **It is** a book **that** I want.
- **It was** yesterday **that** I saw him.

---

### What-cleft

**רגיל:**
- I need more time.

**פיצול:**
- **What** I need **is** more time.
- **What** happened **was** unexpected.

---

## 🔄 Ellipsis (השמטה)

### Omitting Words

**מלא:**
- She can speak English, and he can speak English too.

**מקוצר:**
- She can speak English, and he can **too**.

**מלא:**
- I like coffee, and I like tea.

**מקוצר:**
- I like coffee and tea.

---

## 📝 Advanced Modifiers (מתארים מתקדמים)

### Intensifiers

- **Extremely** important
- **Highly** recommended
- **Utterly** ridiculous
- **Absolutely** certain
- **Completely** wrong
- **Totally** different

---

### Hedging (הסתייגות)

- **Perhaps** / **Maybe** it's true.
- **It seems** that...
- **It appears** that...
- **It might be** that...
- **To some extent**...

---

## 🎓 Academic Structures (מבנים אקדמיים)

### Impersonal Structures

- **It is believed** that...
- **It is argued** that...
- **It is suggested** that...
- **It has been shown** that...
- **It can be seen** that...

---

### Complex Nominalizations

**רגיל:**
- The company **decided** to expand.

**נומינליזציה:**
- The company made a **decision** to expand.

**רגיל:**
- They **analyzed** the data.

**נומינליזציה:**
- They conducted an **analysis** of the data.

---

## 💡 Advanced Punctuation (פיסוק מתקדם)

### Semicolon (נקודה-פסיק)

- I like coffee; she prefers tea.
- The meeting was long; however, it was productive.

---

### Colon (נקודתיים)

- There's one thing I need: your help.
- The reasons are clear: time, money, and effort.

---

### Dash (מקף)

- The solution—which was simple—worked perfectly.
- I need three things—time, money, and patience.

---
