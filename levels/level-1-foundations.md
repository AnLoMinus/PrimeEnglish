## 🗣 Basic Speaking

### Introducing Yourself (הצגה עצמית)

**דיאלוג בסיסי:**

```
A: Hello! My name is David.
B: Hi David! I'm Sarah. Nice to meet you.
A: Nice to meet you too!
B: Where are you from?
A: I'm from Tel Aviv. And you?
B: I'm from Jerusalem.
```

**מילים שימושיות:**
- Hello / Hi - שלום
- My name is... - השם שלי הוא...
- Nice to meet you - נעים להכיר
- Where are you from? - מאיפה אתה?
- I'm from... - אני מ...

### Asking Simple Questions (שאלות פשוטות)

**שאלות בסיסיות:**

1. **What is this?** - מה זה?
   - This is a book.

2. **Who is that?** - מי זה?
   - That is my friend.

3. **Where is...?** - איפה...?
   - Where is the bathroom?

4. **How are you?** - מה שלומך?
   - I'm fine, thank you.

5. **What's your name?** - מה השם שלך?
   - My name is...

### Short Dialogues (דיאלוגים קצרים)

**דיאלוג 1: בחנות**

```
A: Hello! Can I help you?
B: Yes, please. How much is this?
A: It's ten dollars.
B: Thank you!
A: You're welcome!
```

**דיאלוג 2: היכרות**

```
A: Good morning!
B: Good morning! How are you?
A: I'm good, thanks. And you?
B: I'm fine, thank you.
A: What's your name?
B: My name is Tom. What's yours?
A: I'm Lisa.
```

## 📚 Grammar Basics

### Pronouns (כינויי גוף)

| Subject | Object | Possessive |
|---------|--------|------------|
| I | me | my |
| You | you | your |
| He | him | his |
| She | her | her |
| It | it | its |
| We | us | our |
| They | them | their |

**דוגמאות:**
- I am a student. (אני תלמיד)
- This is my book. (זה הספר שלי)
- She is my friend. (היא החברה שלי)

### Simple Present (הווה פשוט)

**שימוש:**
- פעולות קבועות
- הרגלים
- עובדות כלליות

**דוגמאות:**
- I **eat** breakfast every day.
- She **works** in a school.
- They **speak** English.
- The sun **rises** in the east.

**שלילה:**
- I **don't** like coffee.
- She **doesn't** work here.
- They **don't** speak French.

**שאלות:**
- **Do** you like pizza?
- **Does** she work here?
- **Do** they speak English?

### To Be (הפועל להיות)

| Person | Present | Negative |
|--------|---------|----------|
| I | am | am not |
| You | are | are not / aren't |
| He/She/It | is | is not / isn't |
| We | are | are not / aren't |
| They | are | are not / aren't |

**דוגמאות:**
- I **am** a teacher.
- You **are** my friend.
- He **is** tall.
- We **are** students.
- They **are** happy.

### Nouns & Verbs (שמות עצם ופעלים)

**Nouns (שמות עצם):**
- Person: teacher, student, friend
- Place: school, home, city
- Thing: book, car, phone
- Animal: cat, dog, bird

**Verbs (פעלים):**
- Action: run, eat, sleep, read
- State: be, have, like, want

**דוגמאות:**
- The **teacher** (noun) **teaches** (verb) English.
- I **read** (verb) a **book** (noun).
- The **cat** (noun) **sleeps** (verb).
