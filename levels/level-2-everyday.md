# 🔵 Level 2 – Everyday English

**רמה:** מתחילים-בינוניים  
**משך זמן מומלץ:** 6-8 שבועות  
**מטרה:** בניית ביטחון במצבים יומיומיים

## 📋 סקירה כללית

## 🎧 Listening & Speaking

### Short Conversations (שיחות קצרות)

**שיחה 1: בחנות**

```
A: Excuse me, how much is this shirt?
B: It's thirty dollars.
A: Do you have it in blue?
B: Yes, we do. What size?
A: Medium, please.
B: Here you go.
A: Thank you!
```

**שיחה 2: הכוונה**

```
A: Excuse me, where is the train station?
B: Go straight for two blocks, then turn left.
A: Is it far?
B: No, it's about five minutes walk.
A: Thank you very much!
B: You're welcome!
```

### Ordering Food (הזמנת אוכל)

**דיאלוג במסעדה:**

```
Waiter: Good evening! Table for two?
Customer: Yes, please.
Waiter: This way, please.
Waiter: Are you ready to order?
Customer: Yes. I'll have the chicken, please.
Waiter: And to drink?
Customer: Water, please.
Waiter: Anything else?
Customer: No, that's all. Thank you.
```

**מילים שימושיות:**
- Menu - תפריט
- Order - להזמין
- Bill / Check - חשבון
- Tip - טיפ
- Delicious - טעים

### Asking for Help (בקשת עזרה)

**דיאלוגים שימושיים:**

```
A: Excuse me, can you help me?
B: Of course! What do you need?
A: I'm looking for a pharmacy.
B: There's one on Main Street.
A: How do I get there?
B: Turn right at the corner, it's on your left.
A: Thank you so much!
B: No problem!
```

### Daily Routine (שגרה יומית)

**תיאור שגרה:**

```
I wake up at 7 o'clock.
I brush my teeth and take a shower.
I eat breakfast at 8 o'clock.
I go to work at 9 o'clock.
I work until 5 o'clock.
I come home at 6 o'clock.
I eat dinner at 7 o'clock.
I watch TV in the evening.
I go to bed at 11 o'clock.
```

## 🏗 Grammar

### Present Continuous (הווה ממושך)

**שימוש:**
- פעולות שקורות עכשיו
- תוכניות עתידיות

**דוגמאות:**
- I **am reading** a book now.
- She **is working** right now.
- They **are playing** football.
- We **are going** to the cinema tonight.

**שלילה:**
- I **am not** sleeping.
- He **is not** working.
- They **are not** coming.

**שאלות:**
- **Are** you **listening**?
- **Is** she **coming**?
- **What are** you **doing**?

### Past Simple (עבר פשוט)

**שימוש:**
- פעולות שהסתיימו בעבר
- אירועים בעבר

**דוגמאות:**
- I **went** to the store yesterday.
- She **worked** here last year.
- They **played** football last week.
- We **ate** pizza yesterday.

**שלילה:**
- I **didn't** go to school.
- She **didn't** work yesterday.
- They **didn't** come.

**שאלות:**
- **Did** you **go** to the party?
- **Did** she **work** yesterday?
- **What did** you **do**?

**פעלים לא רגילים נפוצים:**
- go → went
- see → saw
- eat → ate
- have → had
- do → did
- come → came
- take → took
- make → made

### Question Forms (צורות שאלה)

**שאלות Yes/No:**
- **Do** you like coffee?
- **Does** she work here?
- **Did** you go yesterday?
- **Are** you ready?
- **Is** she coming?

**שאלות Wh-:**
- **What** do you want?
- **Where** did you go?
- **When** does it start?
- **Who** is that?
- **Why** are you here?
- **How** do you do that?

**שאלות עם How:**
- **How much** does it cost?
- **How many** people are there?
- **How often** do you go?
- **How long** does it take?
