## 🎙 Public Speaking

### Presentations (מצגות)

**מבנה מצגת:**

**1. פתיחה (Introduction):**
```
Good morning, everyone. Thank you for coming.
Today, I'm going to talk about...
I'll cover three main points:
First, ...
Second, ...
Finally, ...
```

**2. גוף (Body):**
```
Let me start with...
Moving on to...
Another important point is...
Finally, I'd like to discuss...
```

**3. סיכום (Conclusion):**
```
To summarize...
In conclusion...
Thank you for your attention.
Are there any questions?
```

**ביטויים שימושיים:**
- I'd like to begin by... - אני רוצה להתחיל ב...
- Let me move on to... - תן לי לעבור ל...
- As you can see... - כפי שאתה יכול לראות...
- This brings me to... - זה מביא אותי ל...
- To wrap up... - לסיכום...

### Pitching (הצגת רעיון)

**דיאלוג pitch:**

```
A: I have an idea I'd like to share.
B: Great, let's hear it.
A: I propose we launch a new product line.
B: What's the benefit?
A: It will increase revenue by 30%.
B: How much will it cost?
A: The initial investment is $50,000.
B: When can we start?
A: We can begin next month.
B: Sounds interesting. Let's discuss the details.
```

**ביטויים שימושיים:**
- I propose... - אני מציע...
- The benefit is... - היתרון הוא...
- The cost is... - העלות היא...
- We can achieve... - אנחנו יכולים להשיג...
- The timeline is... - לוח הזמנים הוא...

### Storytelling (סיפור סיפורים)

**מבנה סיפור:**

```
Once upon a time... / Last year...
At first... / Initially...
Then... / After that...
Suddenly... / Unexpectedly...
Finally... / In the end...
The moral of the story is...
```

**דוגמה:**

```
Last year, I started a new business.
At first, it was very difficult.
Then, I found a great partner.
Suddenly, everything changed.
Finally, we became successful.
The lesson I learned is: never give up.
```

## 📘 Grammar

### Conditionals (תנאים)

**First Conditional (סביר):**
- If + Present Simple, will + verb
- If it rains, I will stay home.
- If you study, you will pass.

**Second Conditional (לא סביר/דמיוני):**
- If + Past Simple, would + verb
- If I won the lottery, I would travel.
- If I were you, I would go.

**Third Conditional (עבר, לא קרה):**
- If + Past Perfect, would have + past participle
- If I had studied, I would have passed.
- If she had come, we would have had fun.

**Zero Conditional (עובדות):**
- If + Present Simple, Present Simple
- If you heat water, it boils.
- If it rains, the ground gets wet.

### Passive Voice (צילום סביל)

**שימוש:**
- כשהפעולה חשובה יותר מהעושה
- כשהעושה לא ידוע או לא חשוב

**דוגמאות:**
- Active: The chef cooks the food.
- Passive: The food **is cooked** by the chef.

- Active: They built this house in 1990.
- Passive: This house **was built** in 1990.

- Active: Someone stole my car.
- Passive: My car **was stolen**.

**זמנים שונים:**
- Present: The letter **is written**.
- Past: The letter **was written**.
- Future: The letter **will be written**.
- Present Perfect: The letter **has been written**.

### Complex Structures (מבנים מורכבים)

**Relative Clauses (פסוקיות יחס):**
- The man **who** called is my friend.
- The book **that** I read was good.
- The place **where** we met is closed.
- The reason **why** I came is important.

**Reported Speech (דיבור עקיף):**
- Direct: "I'm tired," she said.
- Reported: She said **that** she **was** tired.

- Direct: "I will come," he said.
- Reported: He said **that** he **would** come.

**Gerunds & Infinitives:**
- I enjoy **reading** books. (gerund)
- I want **to read** this book. (infinitive)
- I stopped **smoking**. (gerund)
- I stopped **to smoke**. (infinitive - למטרה)
