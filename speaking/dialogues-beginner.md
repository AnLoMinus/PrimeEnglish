# 🗣️ Beginner Dialogues – דיאלוגים למתחילים

**רמה:** Level 1  
**קטגוריה:** דיאלוגים בסיסיים

---

## 👋 Introducing Yourself (הצגה עצמית)

### Dialogue 1: First Meeting

```
A: Hello! My name is David. What's your name?
B: Hi David! I'm Sarah. Nice to meet you.
A: Nice to meet you too, Sarah!
B: Where are you from, David?
A: I'm from Tel Aviv. And you?
B: I'm from Jerusalem. That's nice!
A: Yes, it is. Have a nice day!
B: You too! Goodbye!
```

**מילים חדשות:**
- Nice to meet you - נעים להכיר
- Where are you from? - מאיפה אתה?
- I'm from... - אני מ...
- Goodbye - להתראות

---

### Dialogue 2: At a Party

```
A: Hi! I don't think we've met. I'm Tom.
B: Hello Tom! I'm Lisa. Nice to meet you.
A: Nice to meet you, Lisa. What do you do?
B: I'm a teacher. And you?
A: I'm a student. I study English.
B: That's great! Good luck with your studies!
A: Thank you!
```

**מילים חדשות:**
- What do you do? - מה אתה עושה?
- I'm a... - אני...
- Good luck - בהצלחה

---

## 🛒 Shopping (קניות)

### Dialogue 3: At a Store

```
A: Hello! Can I help you?
B: Yes, please. How much is this shirt?
A: It's twenty dollars.
B: Do you have it in blue?
A: Yes, we do. What size?
B: Medium, please.
A: Here you go.
B: Thank you!
A: You're welcome!
```

**מילים חדשות:**
- Can I help you? - אפשר לעזור?
- How much is...? - כמה זה עולה?
- Size - מידה
- You're welcome - בבקשה

---

### Dialogue 4: Buying Food

```
A: Good morning! What would you like?
B: Good morning! I'd like a coffee, please.
A: Small, medium, or large?
B: Medium, please.
A: Anything else?
B: Yes, a sandwich, please.
A: That's five dollars.
B: Here you are.
A: Thank you! Have a nice day!
B: You too!
```

**מילים חדשות:**
- What would you like? - מה תרצה?
- I'd like... - אני רוצה...
- Anything else? - עוד משהו?

---

## 🗺️ Asking for Directions (בקשת הכוונה)

### Dialogue 5: Finding a Place

```
A: Excuse me, where is the train station?
B: Go straight for two blocks, then turn left.
A: Is it far?
B: No, it's about five minutes walk.
A: Thank you very much!
B: You're welcome!
```

**מילים חדשות:**
- Excuse me - סליחה
- Where is...? - איפה...?
- Go straight - לך ישר
- Turn left - פנה שמאלה
- About - בערך

---

### Dialogue 6: Looking for a Restaurant

```
A: Excuse me, is there a restaurant near here?
B: Yes, there's one on Main Street.
A: How do I get there?
B: Turn right at the corner, it's on your left.
A: Thank you so much!
B: No problem!
```

**מילים חדשות:**
- Is there...? - יש...?
- Near here - קרוב לכאן
- How do I get there? - איך אני מגיע לשם?
- No problem - אין בעיה

## 🍽️ Ordering Food (הזמנת אוכל)

### Dialogue 7: At a Restaurant

```
Waiter: Good evening! Table for two?
Customer: Yes, please.
Waiter: This way, please.
Waiter: Are you ready to order?
Customer: Yes. I'll have the chicken, please.
Waiter: And to drink?
Customer: Water, please.
Waiter: Anything else?
Customer: No, that's all. Thank you.
```

**מילים חדשות:**
- Table for two - שולחן לשניים
- Are you ready to order? - מוכן להזמין?
- I'll have... - אני אקח...
- To drink - לשתות

---

### Dialogue 8: Fast Food

```
A: Welcome! What can I get you?
B: I'd like a hamburger and fries, please.
A: What would you like to drink?
B: A cola, please.
A: For here or to go?
B: To go, please.
A: That's eight dollars.
B: Here you are.
A: Thank you!
```

**מילים חדשות:**
- What can I get you? - מה תרצה?
- For here or to go? - כאן או לקחת?

---

### Dialogue 10: Lost

```
A: Excuse me, I'm lost. Where is the hotel?
B: Which hotel?
A: The Grand Hotel.
B: It's on Park Avenue. Go straight, then turn right.
A: Is it far?
B: No, about ten minutes walk.
A: Thank you!
B: You're welcome!
```

**מילים חדשות:**
- I'm lost - אני הלכתי לאיבוד
- Which...? - איזה...?

## 📞 Phone Conversations (שיחות טלפון)

### Dialogue 11: Answering the Phone

```
A: Hello?
B: Hi, is this Sarah?
A: Yes, this is Sarah. Who's calling?
B: This is Tom. How are you?
A: I'm fine, thanks. And you?
B: I'm good. Can we meet tomorrow?
A: Yes, sure! What time?
B: How about 3 PM?
A: Perfect! See you then!
B: Great! Bye!
```

**מילים חדשות:**
- Who's calling? - מי מתקשר?
- This is... - זה...
- How about...? - מה לגבי...?
- See you then - נתראה אז

## 🎓 At School (בבית ספר)

### Dialogue 12: First Day

```
A: Hi! Are you new here?
B: Yes, I am. My name is Mike.
A: Nice to meet you, Mike! I'm Emma.
B: Nice to meet you too!
A: What class are you in?
B: I'm in class 3A. And you?
A: Me too! Let's go together.
B: Great!
```

**מילים חדשות:**
- Are you new? - אתה חדש?
- What class are you in? - באיזו כיתה אתה?

## 💡 טיפים לתרגול

1. **קרא בקול** - קרא את הדיאלוגים בקול רם
2. **שנה את הפרטים** - החלף שמות ומקומות
3. **תרגל עם חבר** - תרגל עם מישהו אחר
4. **הקלט את עצמך** - הקלט את עצמך מדבר
5. **חזור על זה** - חזור על הדיאלוגים מספר פעמים
