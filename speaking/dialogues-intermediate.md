# 🗣️ Intermediate Dialogues – דיאלוגים בינוניים

**רמה:** Level 2-3  
**קטגוריה:** דיאלוגים בינוניים

---

## 🏢 At Work (בעבודה)

### Dialogue 1: Meeting a Colleague

```
A: Good morning, Sarah! How was your weekend?
B: Good morning, Tom! It was great, thanks. I went to the beach.
A: That sounds nice! I stayed home and relaxed.
B: Sometimes that's the best thing to do.
A: You're right. Are you ready for the meeting?
B: Yes, I am. Let's go.
```

**מילים חדשות:**
- How was your weekend? - איך היה הסוף שבוע?
- That sounds nice - זה נשמע נחמד
- Stayed home - נשארתי בבית
- Relaxed - נחתי

---

### Dialogue 2: Discussing a Project

```
A: Hi, can we talk about the project?
B: Sure, what's on your mind?
A: I think we need more time to finish it.
B: When do you think you can complete it?
A: Maybe next Friday?
B: That should be fine. Let me check the schedule.
A: Thanks, I appreciate it.
B: No problem!
```

**מילים חדשות:**
- What's on your mind? - מה אתה חושב?
- Complete - להשלים
- Schedule - לוח זמנים
- Appreciate - להעריך

---

## 🛫 Travel (נסיעות)

### Dialogue 3: At the Airport

```
A: Good morning! I'd like to check in, please.
B: Good morning! Can I see your passport and ticket?
A: Of course, here you are.
B: Thank you. Window or aisle seat?
A: Window, please.
B: Here's your boarding pass. Have a nice flight!
A: Thank you!
```

**מילים חדשות:**
- Check in - להירשם
- Passport - דרכון
- Ticket - כרטיס
- Window seat - מושב ליד החלון
- Aisle seat - מושב במעבר
- Boarding pass - כרטיס עלייה למטוס

---

### Dialogue 4: At a Hotel

```
A: Good evening! I have a reservation.
B: Good evening! What's your name?
A: John Smith.
B: Let me check... Yes, here it is. For three nights?
A: Yes, that's correct.
B: Here's your key. Room 205, second floor.
A: Thank you! What time is breakfast?
B: From 7 to 10 AM.
A: Perfect, thanks!
```

**מילים חדשות:**
- Reservation - הזמנה
- Correct - נכון
- Key - מפתח
- Floor - קומה
- Breakfast - ארוחת בוקר

---

## 🍽️ Restaurant (מסעדה)

### Dialogue 5: Ordering at a Restaurant

```
Waiter: Good evening! Are you ready to order?
Customer: Yes. I'll have the salmon, please.
Waiter: How would you like it cooked?
Customer: Medium, please.
Waiter: And for the side dish?
Customer: I'll have the vegetables.
Waiter: And to drink?
Customer: A glass of red wine, please.
Waiter: Very good. I'll be back with your order.
```

**מילים חדשות:**
- Salmon - סלמון
- How would you like it cooked? - איך תרצה את זה מבושל?
- Medium - בינוני
- Side dish - תוספת
- Vegetables - ירקות
- Glass of wine - כוס יין

---

### Dialogue 6: Complaining Politely

```
Customer: Excuse me, waiter?
Waiter: Yes, how can I help you?
Customer: I'm sorry, but my soup is cold.
Waiter: I'm so sorry about that. Let me bring you a new one.
Customer: Thank you, I appreciate it.
Waiter: Of course. Is everything else okay?
Customer: Yes, everything else is fine, thank you.
```

**מילים חדשות:**
- Complain - להתלונן
- Cold - קר
- Bring - להביא
- Everything else - כל השאר
- Fine - בסדר

## 🏥 Health (בריאות)

### Dialogue 7: At the Doctor

```
Doctor: Good morning! What seems to be the problem?
Patient: Good morning, doctor. I have a headache and a fever.
Doctor: How long have you had these symptoms?
Patient: Since yesterday morning.
Doctor: Let me check your temperature.
Patient: Okay.
Doctor: You have a slight fever. I'll prescribe some medicine.
Patient: Thank you, doctor.
```

**מילים חדשות:**
- What seems to be the problem? - מה הבעיה?
- Headache - כאב ראש
- Fever - חום
- Symptoms - תסמינים
- Temperature - טמפרטורה
- Slight - קל
- Prescribe - לרשום תרופה
- Medicine - תרופה

---

### Dialogue 8: At the Pharmacy

```
A: Hi, I need to pick up a prescription.
B: What's your name?
A: Sarah Johnson.
B: Let me check... Yes, here it is. That'll be $15.
A: Here you are.
B: Thank you. Take one pill three times a day with food.
A: Got it. Thank you!
B: You're welcome. Feel better!
```

**מילים חדשות:**
- Pick up - לאסוף
- Prescription - מרשם
- Pill - כדור
- Three times a day - שלוש פעמים ביום
- With food - עם אוכל
- Feel better - להרגיש טוב יותר

---

## 🎬 Entertainment (בידור)

### Dialogue 9: Going to the Movies

```
A: What do you want to do tonight?
B: How about going to the movies?
A: That sounds good! What's playing?
B: There's a new action movie at 8 PM.
A: Great! Let's go to that one.
B: Should I buy the tickets online?
A: Yes, that's a good idea. I'll pay you back.
B: No problem!
```

**מילים חדשות:**
- What's playing? - מה מוצג?
- Action movie - סרט פעולה
- Buy tickets - לקנות כרטיסים
- Online - באינטרנט
- Pay back - להחזיר כסף

---

### Dialogue 10: Discussing a Book

```
A: Have you read this book?
B: Yes, I finished it last week. It's really good!
A: What's it about?
B: It's about a detective solving a mystery.
A: That sounds interesting. Is it long?
B: Not too long, about 300 pages.
A: I think I'll read it too.
B: You should! Let me know what you think.
```

**מילים חדשות:**
- Finished - סיימתי
- Detective - בלש
- Solve - לפתור
- Mystery - תעלומה
- Pages - עמודים
- Let me know - תן לי לדעת

---

## 💼 Business (עסקים)

### Dialogue 11: Making an Appointment

```
A: Hello, I'd like to make an appointment.
B: Of course. What day works for you?
A: How about next Tuesday?
B: Let me check... Tuesday at 2 PM is available.
A: Perfect! That works for me.
B: Great! I'll see you then.
A: Thank you! Goodbye!
```

**מילים חדשות:**
- Make an appointment - לקבוע פגישה
- What day works for you? - איזה יום מתאים לך?
- Available - זמין
- Works for me - מתאים לי

---

### Dialogue 12: Negotiating

```
A: I'd like to discuss the price.
B: Of course. What did you have in mind?
A: Can you offer a discount?
B: I can give you 10% off.
A: What about 15%?
B: I can do 12%, that's my best offer.
A: That sounds fair. Let's do it.
B: Great! When can you start?
```

**מילים חדשות:**
- Discuss - לדון
- Offer - להציע
- Discount - הנחה
- Best offer - ההצעה הטובה ביותר
- Fair - הוגן

---

## 💡 טיפים לתרגול

1. **הרחב את אוצר המילים** - הוסף מילים חדשות
2. **שנה את הנושא** - נסה נושאים שונים
3. **השתמש בזמנים שונים** - עבר, הווה, עתיד
4. **תרגל עם דוברי אנגלית** - שוחח עם אחרים
5. **הקלט את עצמך** - הקלט ובדוק את ההגייה
